Teaching

Courses, guest lectures and student support in urban economics, economic geography and applied quantitative methods.

Since 2024 I have been Lecturer in Urban Economics at the Africa Urban Lab of the African School of Economics in Zanzibar, where I co-created and teach the urban economics module of its Professional Diploma for mid-career urban professionals from across Africa. I also teach urban economics to undergraduates at LSE, and co-convened a global PhD course in urban economics. In 2026 I received the Class Teacher Award in LSE’s Department of Geography and Environment, having been nominated by the LSE Students’ Union for Outstanding Teacher of the Year the year before.

Teaching interests

Urban and regional economics; global value chains and foreign direct investment; economic geography; the political economy of development and of local government; and applied quantitative methods for development, including causal inference with administrative and spatial data and text-as-data.
