Harnessing Global Value Chains for Regional Development

How to Upgrade through Regional Policy, FDI and Trade

The 2023 Regional Studies Policy Impact Book by Riccardo Crescenzi and Oliver Harman, with its reviews, the policy documents that cite it, and the governments and agencies that have used it.

Riccardo Crescenzi and I wrote this book for the people who have to make regional policy decisions. It asks what a region can realistically do about its position in global production, and it works through the levers a regional government holds, which are its own policy, the foreign investment it attracts and the trade it carries. Routledge published it in 2023 in the Regional Studies Policy Impact Book series, and the overview chapter is open access.

Since publication the book has been reviewed in three journals and in the financial press, cited in policy documents from the World Bank, the OECD and the United Nations, used in evidence to two House of Commons committees and to the Harrington Review, and worked through with investment promotion agencies in Asia, Africa and Europe. What follows is that record, drawn from the book’s impact and engagement report of May 2025.

Reviews

Crescenzi and Harman have crafted an important work that bridges theory with actionable insights, offering a roadmap for regions keen on leveraging GVCs for sustainable growth.

Ricardo Hausmann, Harvard Kennedy School, in Regional Studies

A book that does a great job presenting the opportunities of GVC integration. It is highly valuable, not only for policy makers but also for researchers and students.

Sören Scholvin, University of Cologne, in Economic Geography (2023)

The book provides tools and insights for policy-makers to navigate the changing landscape of value chains and regional development.

Syahputra Rambe, Sulistiani and Rambasae, in European Planning Studies (2024)

The Financial Times’ fDi Intelligence reviewed the book as a new template for regions to harness global value chains, and LSE listed it among its recommended reads for understanding the economy today.

Used by governments and agencies

  • Ministries across West and East Africa A presentation on infrastructure for African integration into global value chains, to officials from the ministries responsible for economy, finance and trade in Côte d’Ivoire, Djibouti, Senegal and Togo

  • Connected Places Catapult An applied toolkit for local governments in the North of England, Leveraging Opportunities from Global Value Chains for Regional Development

  • UNU-WIDER and the African Cities Research Consortium Findings presented at a workshop on African urbanisation and structural transformation
