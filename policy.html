Policy research

Policy research on municipal finance, global value chains and urban sustainability, written for governments in low-resource settings.

For a decade I wrote for and worked alongside finance ministries, city treasuries and mayoral teams across Africa, Asia and the Caribbean, mostly through the International Growth Centre’s Cities that Work initiative, and before that as an economist embedded in Guyana’s Ministry of Communities. That work has 61 externally validated instances of uptake by decision-makers, across 27 countries and four international organisations, a measure audited by the IGC’s government funder. The record is below.

The work below was written to be used rather than cited. Several pieces exist in French, Somali, Arabic or Hindi; ask if you need one.

Government of Guyana

Before joining the IGC I was embedded in Guyana’s Ministry of Communities, working closely with the Minister, Permanent Secretary and Head of Planning. Guyana held its first local government elections for 20 years in 2016, and I helped build local government capacity, particularly through municipal finance reform.

  • Economist, then Senior Economist and ODI Fellow, Ministry of Communities (2016–2018) Input to the Green State Development Strategy (Office of the President); Addressing the Challenges Facing the Government Housing Programme (with the Central Housing and Planning Authority and Ministry of Finance); municipal tax progression, local government renewable energy policy, economic profiles of Guyana’s capital towns, and public financial management tools for municipalities

European Commission with UN-Habitat, with the IGC as academic partner co-creating and co-delivering the programme: a high-level policy session at the European Parliament in Brussels, a cities and experts workshop in Dakar following the European Investment Bank’s Africa Day, and the case studies above Collection European Parliament session Dakar workshop
